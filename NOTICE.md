# Legal Notice & Data Provenance

The Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license in `LICENSE` applies to the Licensed Material in this repository, defined as the public market notes, audit reports, processed metrics, figures, and analysis scripts.

## Third-Party Data Exclusions

Third-party raw market data, telemetry, and official disclosures (including disclosures from ENTSO-E, Energinet, and other European Transmission System Operators) are public utility disclosures governed by their respective licenses (e.g., ENTSO-E CC BY 4.0, Energinet CC BY 4.0).

* **No Relicensing:** Third-party raw data is explicitly excluded from our license grant. We do not relicense market operator disclosures.
* **No Raw Data Storage in Repository:** Raw payloads returned by third-party APIs used for cross-validation and audit testing are not checked into this git repository. Provenance is maintained via machine-readable SHA-256 manifests (`data_manifest.json`) and deterministic reproduction harnesses.
